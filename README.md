# All vibe no cry

**Vibe fast. Ship safe.**

From vibe to live app: a self-hosted suite that lets anyone build, publish and
run their own web apps with AI on an old computer at home, without a terminal
and without losing data.

> **In development. Not ready to use.** Nothing here is ready to install on a
> machine that matters. Follow along at [allvibenocry.com](https://allvibenocry.com).

## What it will do

- Turn an old PC or a VM running Debian 13 into a home for your apps.
- Give every app two copies: **dev**, where an AI coding agent builds, and
  **prod**, with your real data, which the agent can never reach.
- Refuse to release anything without a fresh backup, off the machine, that has
  been proven to restore.
- Roll back on its own when a new version does not work, without losing your
  data.
- Let you do all of it from a browser.
