# Friction log

Kept by the owner while building the suite's first real project, the homelab
documentation site ([roadmap](roadmap.md), D54): every place where the suite got
in the way, was confusing, or was missing something. It feeds the briefs that
follow. Nothing here is a decision; the decisions are in
[DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md).

One entry per snag, newest first:

- **Date, and where** (the step, the screen or the command)
- **What I wanted to do**
- **What happened instead**, in plain words
- **What I did about it** (a workaround, or nothing)
- **How much it hurt**: a little, a lot, or it stopped me
